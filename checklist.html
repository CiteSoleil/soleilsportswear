Tester checklist

Go through each section on the site, then mark every check Pass, Fail or Skip. Add a note to anything that fails. When you are done, send the results at the bottom. Your marks are saved on this device until you clear them.

Before you start

  1. Open the site in a second tab with the link at the top.
  2. Run the whole list once on a phone and once on a computer, if you can.
  3. Run it once in light mode and once in dark mode (your device's appearance setting).
  4. Turn your sound on. Music and shop sounds are part of the test.
  5. Ask Stevens for the access code. Do not share it.
  6. To test the lock again from scratch, use "Reset site memory" at the bottom of this page.
